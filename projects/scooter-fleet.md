# Campus Electric Scooter Fleet Rollout

**Domain:** Systems Logistics | Fleet Sizing | Network Planning  
**Tools & Frameworks:** Operations Research, Queuing Theory, Python / MATLAB, Spatial Optimization

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Campus micro-mobility programs face high operational friction due to uneven fleet distribution, battery depletion, and uncoordinated rebalancing. Without optimized fleet sizing and hub placement, peak-hour availability drops, leading to unmet user demand and excessive repositioning costs.

---

## 2. Methodology & Tools Applied
* **Fleet Sizing & Demands Analysis:** Applied queuing models and stochastic demand estimation across peak campus transit windows.
* **Hub Network Planning:** Formulated a spatial optimization model to determine optimal charging station and drop-off hub locations across campus nodes.
* **Operational Modeling:** Simulated rebalancing schedules and battery swapping routines to balance operational cost against service level targets.
